CCDI Campus Siomai


Welcome to CCDI Campus Siomai, proudly serving the students, teachers, and visitors of CCDI in Sikatuna, Old Albay District, Legazpi City, Albay! At Campus Siomai, we are passionate about providing delicious, freshly made siomai meals that are perfect for the fast-paced life of the school community. Our siomai is carefully steamed to perfection every day to ensure the best taste and quality. Whether you're looking for a light snack or a full meal, we've got you covered with affordable options that are easy on the pocket.

We offer:

  • Siomai — only 5 pesos per piece
  • Siopao — only 10 pesos per serving
  • Rice and Siomai Combo — only 10 pesos per serving
  • Water Bottle — just 15 pesos
  • Cola-Cola — just 25 pesos
  • HOT DOG — just 15 pesos

Our mission is simple: to deliver tasty, satisfying, and budget-friendly meals that give students the energy they need to succeed and enjoy their day. We understand the busy life inside the school, so we make sure our service is fast, friendly, and always ready when you need a quick bite between classes or a hearty lunch. What makes Campus Siomai special is our dedication to quality, affordability, and the comfort of our CCDI family. We don't just serve food — we serve a taste of home, comfort, and community inside the campus. Visit us at the canteen today and experience why Campus Siomai is a favorite stop for many! Thank you for being part of our growing family. Your support inspires us to continue serving better every day.
